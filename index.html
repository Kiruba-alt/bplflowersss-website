How a shipment moves

Six checkpoints,
one unbroken cold chain.

Every order is tracked through six stages, each logged with a timestamp and a temperature reading you can audit on delivery.

01
Hour 0 · 4°C

Harvest

Stems cut at optimal bud stage, early morning.

02
Hour 2 · 2°C

Grading

Sorted by stem length, bud count and colour grade.

03
Hour 5 · 2°C

Cold Storage

Hydrated and held in pre-cool rooms before dispatch.

04
Hour 10 · 4°C

Airfreight

Palletised in refrigerated cargo to destination hub.

05
Hour 30 · 4°C

Customs & Inspection

Phytosanitary clearance handled by our local agents.

06
Hour 46 · 4°C

Delivery

Last-mile delivery to your cold room, signed and logged.
